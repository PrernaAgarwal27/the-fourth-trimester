LET'S TALK ABOUT THE FOURTH TRIMESTER

The care after birth
matters just as much.

The first three months after childbirth can bring physical recovery, changes in sleep, feeding questions, shifting relationships and a whole new identity. There is no single way to experience this time.

Good information and a caring support system can help families prepare, share responsibilities and notice when extra help is needed. Our goal is to make postpartum support easier to understand and easier to ask for.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Who are these workshops for?

They are designed for new and expectant mothers, partners, fathers and family members who want to understand the postpartum period and how to support it. Add specific eligibility details for each workshop before launch.

Are these sessions medical care or therapy?

No. The Fourth Trimester provides educational information and practical support, not diagnosis, medical treatment or psychotherapy. Please speak with a qualified healthcare professional about individual medical or mental health concerns.

Can my partner or a family member join?

Yes, where the session format allows. Family members can learn practical ways to share responsibilities and support the mother's recovery and wellbeing.

How do I book and pay?

Use the booking links on this page once they have been activated. You will receive the relevant details and payment instructions from the selected booking provider.

What if I feel overwhelmed or unsafe?

You deserve timely support. Contact your healthcare provider or local mental health service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency service.
